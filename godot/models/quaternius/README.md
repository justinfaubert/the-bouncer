# Quaternius: the borrowed bodies and motion

CC0 (public domain), by Quaternius (quaternius.com), from the free Standard packs Hutch downloaded
on 18 November 2026. See `LICENSE_CC0.txt`.

- `ual1.glb`: the Universal Animation Library, 43 clips on the UE-mannequin skeleton (idle,
  talking, sitting and talking, walking, interact, pick up, push, punches, hits, death...). No root
  motion; `UAL1_Standard_RM` (root motion baked) wasn't brought in.
- `base_male.glb`, `base_female.glb`: the Universal Base Characters' two bodies on the same
  skeleton, their textures dropped (the print paints everything by material).

`tests/shots_motion.gd` plays them, printed, on twos. Part of the people's new direction
(PROPOSED): borrowed motion, bespoke bodies, drawn faces.
