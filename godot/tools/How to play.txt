THE BOUNCER
Wednesday, the test shift. The Godot build.

Linux:   mark it runnable once (chmod +x TheBouncer.x86_64), then run it.
Windows: run TheBouncer.exe. It isn't signed, so Windows asks first: More info, then Run anyway.

It runs on OpenGL 3.3, so any graphics card from the last ten years will do.

Choose            click a choice, or press its number
Go on             Space or Enter, where the night stops to let a moment land
Look round        drag across the room, or the arrow keys (or W A S D); the wheel narrows his eyes
Look at a thing   rest the pointer on what a choice is about: the choice lights up; E or a click does it
Throw the dice    hold Space or the mouse button to shake, let go to throw
In his hands      drag to turn it, E to look closer, Q and R to go through it, Esc to put it down
The clicker       C clicks it; hold Z to turn the knob back to nothing
Notes, sketch     Tab, M
Pause, settings   Esc. Every choice is saved as it's made; there are three nights.
On a pad          either stick looks round; the d-pad goes through the choices and A makes one

Saves and settings are kept in ~/.local/share/The Bouncer/ on Linux and %APPDATA%\The Bouncer\ on
Windows.

Every sound is a placeholder made in code. A .wav, .ogg or .mp3 with the same name in a folder
called "audio" next to the game takes its place from the next start; more takes of one sound go in
as click_2, click_3 and so on. The names:

  the room      amb_rain_glass amb_rain_out amb_rain_roof amb_room_tone amb_talk_a amb_talk_b
                amb_shout amb_juke amb_pool amb_fryer amb_fan amb_tube amb_cellar amb_rats amb_sea
  things        click (the clicker) ratchet (its knob) zero beep_ok beep_bad (the scanner)
                thump (the door) gust rattle tick tock (the dice) clack (the balls) thud ring
                glass scratch till bell card page pen printer swipe tear stamp steps heart
                whisper lift choose click_ui
