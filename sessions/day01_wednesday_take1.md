## Log

### Opening (Claude)

He started on a Wednesday. The Sov tried out doormen on Wednesdays, on a night slow enough that a mistake would only hurt a little. Of the thirty days, it was the one nobody else remembered.

7:40 p.m. Rain on the gravel lot. Inside: beer, cleaner, piss, old wood, dust. The bartender slides him a clicker across the bar. "Count them in. Count them out. The owner left you a note." Taped to the till, in marker: RATS. DEAL WITH IT. INSPECTOR ON THE 14TH.

Table 28 is five men tonight, talking about the price of diesel in one voice. Behind them, something scratches inside the wall. One of them drops a fry under the table without looking down.

THE FATHER: Count the room before it counts you. Those five already think they own you.
THE FIGHTER: Big one at the pool table. Watch his left hand.
THE MANUAL: The licensee's duty of care begins at the threshold.

At the door: a kid in a soaked hoodie, earbuds in, holding out a phone with a photo of an ID instead of the card. Behind him in the lot, a pickup idles with its lights on and two more shapes inside.
