## What a meek man changes first

The voice sheet's rules, applied to what's here. These are the lines most written for a hard woman:

- **Orders.** She tells him ("You don't run on my floor."). He asks, sorry first, and doesn't wait for the answer.
- **Names for customers.** *Hon* and *sweetheart* are hers; the lines are below. He says *folks*, or nothing.
- **Junior.** She calls the owner that behind his back because she worked for his father. Whether Jim does is the rewrite's call.
- **Her years.** "Thirty-one years" and her start as a server at nineteen are hers; his years, and the band, come with the rewrite.
- **She.** The narration below.
